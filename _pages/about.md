I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics](https://are.ucdavis.edu/) at UC Davis, co-advised by Profs. Pierre Mérel and Richard J. Sexton. I expect to complete my PhD in June 2027. My research lies at the intersection of agricultural economics and international trade, with a secondary field in industrial organization.

My [job market paper](https://cliobserver.github.io/zhiran-AgEcon.github.io/publication/2025-07-01-nutrition-transition) studies how the nutrition transition, the dietary shift from starchy staples toward vegetables, fruits, and animal protein as developing countries become richer, combines with population growth to reshape global land use and food prices through 2050. My published work examines how policy-driven organic farmland expansion in high-income countries shapes global food prices and welfare. In ongoing projects, I quantify the global market effects of China's commercialization of genetically modified corn and soybeans (with Zhiyao Ma and Pierre Mérel), and study how buyers' capacity constraints attenuate competition and depress contract prices in agricultural procurement markets (with Richard J. Sexton).

Before coming to Davis, I completed my undergraduate studies in Agricultural Economy and Management at Renmin University of China.
