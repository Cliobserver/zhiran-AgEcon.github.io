With Richard J. Sexton. Using duopsony examples and a Hotelling framework, we show that binding capacity constraints severely attenuate competitive bidding in agricultural procurement markets, pushing contract prices toward sellers' outside options even when multiple non-collusive buyers are present. The analysis identifies capacity constraints as an underappreciated channel of buyer market power, with implications for farm prices, farmer welfare, and antitrust policy.
