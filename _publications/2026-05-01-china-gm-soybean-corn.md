With Zhiyao Ma and Pierre Mérel. China's move from pilot trials to nationwide commercialization of genetically modified corn and soybeans marks a potentially consequential shift in global crop supply. We develop and calibrate a partial equilibrium model of world crop supply, demand, and trade to quantify how this domestic rollout transmits to international markets. A 20% yield gain on China's soybean acreage raises domestic output substantially but transmits weakly to world prices; welfare gains concentrate in China, while Brazil and the United States bear the largest producer losses.
