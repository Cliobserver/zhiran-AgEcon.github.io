Presented my job market paper at the 2025 AAEA &amp; WAEA Joint Annual Meeting. The paper develops a partial equilibrium model of global crop and food markets to project country-specific food prices, crop prices, and land use to 2050 under alternative nutrition-transition and population-growth scenarios.
